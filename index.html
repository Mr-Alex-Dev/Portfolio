We define the business problem, the users and what “done” means. I ask the uncomfortable questions before writing code.

Design

Data model, information architecture and a visual direction you approve before implementation starts. No surprises later.

04 — About

A developer who treats your business software like a product.

I build web applications the way I would want to use them: fast, honest and maintainable. My flagship projects — a full CRM and a live business website — were designed, engineered and tested by me end to end, which is exactly how I work with clients.

My strength is the full path: understanding the business process, designing the data model, implementing a secure backend, polishing the interface and verifying everything with tests. I document what I build, so your team is never locked into me.

I work remotely with clients worldwide, in English, and I keep communication simple: clear scope, visible progress, and working software you can click on — not status screenshots.
